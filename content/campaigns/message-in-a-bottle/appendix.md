## NPC profiles

All profiles below are original builds for this edition. Attributes, skills, Pace, Parry, and Toughness use SWADE conventions. **Wild Card** and **Extra** are rules labels; use the core book for how each works. Unlisted skills use the core unskilled procedure. Damage marked **Str+d4** or **Str+d6** uses the character's Strength die. Armor is included in Toughness and shown in parentheses. Listed gear is deliberately simple; substitute equipment from your own core book when appropriate.

### Mark, returning hero - Wild Card

Use only if Mark is not a player character. He is glad to be home, frightened of going back outside, and unwilling to treat the displaced as disposable.

**Attributes:** Agility d6, Smarts d8, Spirit d8, Strength d6, Vigor d6. **Skills:** Athletics d6, Common Knowledge d6, Electronics d8, Fighting d4, Notice d6, Persuasion d6, Repair d6, Stealth d4. **Pace:** 6. **Parry:** 4. **Toughness:** 5. **Hindrances:** Loyal. **Edges:** None. **Gear:** A scorched coat, insulated tools, a pocket notebook; improvised tool, Str+d4.

**Navigation fiction:** Mark can feed his electrical current into a compatible device. This is not a general combat power or a new Arcane Background. Roll his Electronics when he operates that device. He can Support; he cannot replace a player's action. If a player wants a fully powered Mark, agree a core-rules character build and Trappings separately. No proprietary power text is included here.

### Contractor, security operative - Extra

A worker following an emergency contract, not automatically a murderer. Most surrender or retreat when abandoned. A persuaded crew may become an ally.

**Attributes:** Agility d6, Smarts d4, Spirit d6, Strength d6, Vigor d6. **Skills:** Athletics d6, Fighting d6, Notice d6, Persuasion d4, Repair d6, Shooting d6, Stealth d4. **Pace:** 6. **Parry:** 5. **Toughness:** 6 (1). **Hindrances/Edges:** None. **Gear:** Protective vest, Armor 1; tool baton, Str+d4; insulated net, use a core grappling or improvised restraint resolution. No firearm is required for any scene in this campaign.

### Custodian, dimensional security - Extra

A person paid to protect a jurisdiction they only partly understand. Their priority is their own exit route, then their assigned checkpoint.

**Attributes:** Agility d6, Smarts d6, Spirit d4, Strength d8, Vigor d8. **Skills:** Athletics d6, Fighting d6, Intimidation d6, Notice d6, Repair d4. **Pace:** 6. **Parry:** 5. **Toughness:** 8 (2). **Hindrances/Edges:** None. **Gear:** Protective coat, Armor 2; heavy baton, Str+d4; transit badge. **Tactic:** Guard an exit, warn once, and withdraw if the contract is visibly cancelled. Persuasion or a convincing forged order can end the encounter without fighting.

### Rift leech - Extra

An original creature resembling a wasp assembled from electrical insulation. Treat its tangled cable mass as Size 0 for this profile. It feeds on current, not on moral alignment. A spare battery can draw it away.

**Attributes:** Agility d8, Smarts d4 (animal), Spirit d6, Strength d4, Vigor d4. **Skills:** Athletics d8, Fighting d6, Notice d6, Stealth d8. **Pace:** 6 (flight). **Parry:** 5. **Toughness:** 4. **Hindrances/Edges:** None. **Gear:** None. **Attack:** Bite, Str+d4. **Custom ability:** While a leech is attached to a live cable, that feed cannot contribute to a task. An adjacent character may remove it with a successful Athletics or Repair roll as an action, or lure it away with another live source without a roll. It has no unexplained immunity or copied creature abilities.

### Calder, fictional mayor - Wild Card

This is a fictional antagonist, not a claim about a real politician or family. He wants ordinary status and unlimited control over the disasters that make him look useful.

**Attributes:** Agility d6, Smarts d8, Spirit d8, Strength d6, Vigor d6. **Skills:** Athletics d4, Common Knowledge d8, Electronics d6, Fighting d4, Intimidation d6, Notice d6, Persuasion d8, Repair d6, Research d6, Stealth d4. **Pace:** 6. **Parry:** 4. **Toughness:** 5. **Hindrances:** Greedy (Major). **Edges:** None. **Gear:** Auction key, emergency procurement book, portable radio; improvised briefcase attack, Str+d4. **Tactic:** Offer jobs, invoke procedure, retreat behind contractors. He is not a super-powered combat boss.

### The repossessor - Wild Card

The Keeper's field agent. Courteous, precise, and incapable of seeing a resident as anything but inventory until someone makes that assumption costly.

**Attributes:** Agility d8, Smarts d10, Spirit d8, Strength d6, Vigor d8. **Skills:** Athletics d6, Common Knowledge d8, Electronics d8, Fighting d8, Intimidation d8, Notice d8, Persuasion d8, Repair d6, Research d8, Stealth d6. **Pace:** 6. **Parry:** 6. **Toughness:** 8 (2). **Hindrances/Edges:** None. **Gear:** Boundary coat, Armor 2; tuning rod, Str+d6; a portable jurisdiction stamp.

**Custom jurisdiction anchor:** The rod sustains one visible tether attached to the vessel. An adjacent hero can disable it with Electronics or Repair at -2, break the exposed terminal (Toughness 6), or get the agent to release it. Disabling the tether does not require defeating the agent. The agent is not immune to ordinary combat or Tests. It leaves if the boundary is severed and it has no defensible contract.

### Allies and the Keeper

The records clerk **Inez**, cartoon resident **Pip**, and engineer **Dr. Vale** are original supporting names for this edition. Give an ally d6 in a relevant skill when a roll is needed; they supply access, context, or a single Support action, not Task Tokens on behalf of an absent player. Pip does not have to be comic relief.

The Vivarium Keeper is a remote antagonist. Do not give it a huge combat profile and turn the ending into a fight with an unkillable creature. Its actions arrive through a shake, a contract, a tether, or an agent: specific things the heroes can confront. Its baseline is a collector who calls danger enrichment. If your table prefers the Cruel Naturalist or the Spectator, change the voice and motive, not the established clues or shaking rights.

## Table reference

### The Thompson Center node map

This is a fictional encounter map, not a plan of the real building. Each connection takes one scene transition; do not measure a real pedestrian route.

| Node | Exits | Question or pressure |
| --- | --- | --- |
| A - Ticket hall | B, C | Whose jurisdiction is this? |
| B - Elven departures | A, D | Save stranded travelers or accept a shortcut? |
| C - Municipal records | A, D | Who is paid, and who is named as owner? |
| D - Weatherization floor | B, C, E | Disable the shake controls or follow Calder? |
| E - Control seam | D; accelerator route | What do the heroes need to free the city? |

A closed route always has the other listed route. A clerk can redirect a lost group to C. The maze's shift clock creates a cost, not an infinite random walk. The GM runner tracks scene completion independently of clock pressure.

### Pressure ledger

| Clock | Boxes | Fill a box when | At the limit |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Portal instability | 3 | A full tutorial task round ends below the goal | Mark arrives with collateral trouble |
| Public danger | 6 | A rescue round ends with nobody addressing civilians | Another group needs rescue; do not silently kill them |
| Maze shift | 4 | The group repeats a route without changing method, or accepts a listed delay | Custodians arrive; the exit remains reachable |
| Earthquake | 6 | An accelerator round ends; add one extra on a stated containment mishap | Ground hazard and evacuation; firing remains possible |
| Slide departure | 6 | A travel beat finishes, including rescue or repairs | Window closes; use the missed-window route |
| Final tether | 4 | A finale task round ends while the tether remains | The agent boards; keep the final objective available |

These are manual scene clocks, not actual-minute timers. The runner's session timer is a pacing aid and does not fill them automatically.

### Encounter scaling and review

Start with one contractor per hero in scenes that call for contractors; use half that many custodians, rounded up, when a threat also shares a timer. Add the repossessor only at the finale. Never add every possible enemy from the appendix to one scene.

Run one dry pass before inviting a table: follow the clues without rolls, take the failure exit from the portal, skip one travel window, and end with the tether intact. The campaign must still have a next action in every case. That structural check is not a playtest. Damage balance, actual pacing, and the emotional effect of the ending require people playing.

### Between gatherings

- Write down the revealed clues, chosen costs, surviving allies, and one thing a player made true.
- Ask what destination or investigation the group wants next. Prepare it before filling in an unrelated branch.
- Record any content adjustment privately. Player personal disclosures do not belong in the public module or source repository.
- Advance only at the agreed milestones. Update encounters for the characters' actual capabilities.
- Keep the next session's opening, immediate objective, essential clue, and two exit states within reach.
