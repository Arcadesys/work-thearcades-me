## 1 - Reunion sheet

**Player sheet - safe before the campaign begins.**

Chicago has had ten quiet years. A friend is coming home by an unusual route. Your crew has a portal to build and a city you remember saving.

Choose or invent together:

- Your hero's name and what they do now.
- One place in Chicago worth saving.
- One person your hero would ask for help.
- One strange image from the old adventures. This is a proposal until the group confirms it.
- What your hero is willing to risk to bring a friend home.

Keep private boundaries in a separate conversation with the GM. You do not owe this sheet a personal confession. New groups can invent a shared past; returning groups can leave uncertain memories uncertain.

THIRD PULSE - the slide opens. Establish an anchor at both ends.

WINDOW CLOSED - find the next crossing. Missing one passage is not the end of the voyage.

HOME SIGNAL - three long flashes, one short flash, and the name of a place the crew chose to save.

Never promise the Keeper a new wall around the city in exchange for an easier trip.
