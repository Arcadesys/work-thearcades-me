# Building internal tools and moving a blocked initiative forward

Canonical page: https://work.thearcades.me/work/guaranteed-rate

## Summary

At Guaranteed Rate, Austen Tucker-Crowder shipped internal software and later acted as product owner on a separate data-mining initiative associated with roughly $1.5 billion in locked loan volume.

## Austen's role

Scrum Master and project lead; later product owner on the data-mining initiative.

## Context

Guaranteed Rate, 2015–2018.

## Evidence

- Austen learned JavaScript and shipped internal tools, including a real-time lobby display of loan locks.
- Austen later acted as product owner on a separate data-mining initiative associated with roughly $1.5B in locked loan volume.
- Small shipped artifacts created feedback and credibility faster than abstract plans.

The lobby display and the $1.5B data-mining initiative were separate pieces of work. The volume figure describes the initiative's associated locked loans, not revenue Austen personally generated.

## The problem

Austen joined an engineering-driven environment where credibility came from shipping and where a major initiative later became blocked through normal leadership channels.

## What Austen owned

Austen built the internal loan-lock display while doing coordination work. On the separate data-mining initiative, Austen acted as product owner and helped secure the executive sponsorship needed to move blocked work forward.

## What changed

The work established a pattern Austen continues to use: build the smallest useful thing, get it into people's hands quickly, and use feedback to decide what deserves to exist next.

## Principle

Build small. Build simple. Get feedback.
