Message in a Bottle

Reunion sheet, discovered documents, route card, and session record.

For Savage Worlds Adventure Edition
Campaign by Austen Tucker-Crowder

Requires the SWADE core rules. Unofficial, unplaytested fan edition. AI assistance disclosed in the credits.

1 - Reunion sheet

Player sheet - safe before the campaign begins.

Chicago has had ten quiet years. A friend is coming home by an unusual route. Your crew has a portal to build and a city you remember saving.

Choose or invent together:

  • Your hero's name and what they do now.
  • One place in Chicago worth saving.
  • One person your hero would ask for help.
  • One strange image from the old adventures. This is a proposal until the group confirms it.
  • What your hero is willing to risk to bring a friend home.

Keep private boundaries in a separate conversation with the GM. You do not owe this sheet a personal confession. New groups can invent a shared past; returning groups can leave uncertain memories uncertain.

2 - Emergency procurement notice

Reveal in Session 2.

CITY EMERGENCY REPAIR DESK

Job: physical-law stabilization, corridor containment, and post-event weatherization.

Authorized contractor: Calder Restoration.

Replacement panels delivered: BEFORE INCIDENT.

Emergency surcharge approved: AFTER INCIDENT.

Routing mark: THOMPSON / ENVIRONMENTAL CONTROL.

Clerk's note: Why do they always know which panels to bring?

3 - Auction compensation clause

Reveal in Session 4. Full spoilers after this point.

ESTATE OF UP - COLLECTION DISPOSAL

Lot: inhabited Chicago holding.

Eligible bidder: Vivarium Keeper.

Human beneficiary: Calder.

Consideration to bidder: use of holding as a living snow globe, including periodic enrichment by shaking.

Resident consent: not requested.

Maintenance: sufficient viable population to continue the arrangement.

Control interface: Thompson / weatherization floor / seam terminal.

Annotation: An entry in this ledger does not make a person a thing.

4 - Three-pulse route card

Reveal in Session 6.

FIRST PULSE - a neighboring reality is approaching.

SECOND PULSE - prepare the vessel and finish the local choice.

THIRD PULSE - the slide opens. Establish an anchor at both ends.

WINDOW CLOSED - find the next crossing. Missing one passage is not the end of the voyage.

HOME SIGNAL - three long flashes, one short flash, and the name of a place the crew chose to save.

Never promise the Keeper a new wall around the city in exchange for an easier trip.
